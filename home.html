What We Do

We build the US financial foundation international founders shouldn't have to figure out alone.

American Leverage exists because the US financial system is the most powerful operating infrastructure in the world, and most international founders are locked out of it by default.

A properly registered LLC, a real US business bank account at Chase or Bank of America, and a structure that holds up under review. For an American founder these are unremarkable starting points. For an international entrepreneur, they have historically been nearly impossible to access without the right framework.

We close that gap. We give international founders the filings, the frameworks, and the direct support to enter the US financial system correctly, and stay there for the long term.

Every setup we run is built to last. No nominee addresses. No shortcuts that trigger account freezes six months in. Real banking relationships, established properly, that grow in standing over time.

  • Legitimate from day one

    Every setup follows proper US legal and banking requirements. No workarounds, no strategies that depend on loopholes. Built to survive a bank review now and five years from now.

  • Built for operations, not paperwork

    The goal is a working US business infrastructure: accounts you can actually use, a structure that stands up, and banking relationships you can grow on over time.

  • Direct banking experience

    We have walked hundreds of non-resident founders through the exact process US banks use to evaluate international accounts. We know what works and what gets flagged.

  • For founders already in motion

    This is for operators running real businesses who want to expand inside the world's largest market. Not beginners. Not people without revenue. Founders ready to compete on equal footing.

Jean-Paul D'Andrea

Known to clients and the community simply as JP.

Jean-Paul D'Andrea built American Leverage after identifying a structural problem at the intersection of international entrepreneurship and US finance: the entire operating infrastructure that American founders take for granted is essentially invisible to everyone else.

Founders from Germany, Australia, the UAE, and dozens of other countries were running real, serious businesses, and repeatedly hitting the same wall when they tried to access the US financial system. Not because the door was permanently closed, but because nobody had shown them how to open it correctly.

JP spent years working through the bank relationships, legal frameworks, and compliance requirements that actually unlock US business banking for non-residents. He turned that work into a methodology his team now applies for founders across more than 30 countries: a systematic, legitimate path into the system that produces real, durable results.

To date, that work has produced over 500 completed US setups: working bank accounts at Chase, Bank of America, and Amex; properly structured LLCs; and the banking foundation international founders need to operate at the level their businesses deserve.

JP also runs the Skool community that has grown to over 6,100 members, a network of founders from around the world who are building their US presence correctly, sharing what works, and supporting each other through the process.
