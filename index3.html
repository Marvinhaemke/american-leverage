Ready To Take the First Step? Get Your ITIN.

Here's what most non-U.S. business owners don't realize: you can't build real American credit without an ITIN. It's your identifier in the U.S. system, the foundation that lets you open bank accounts, apply for credit cards, and access the same 0% financing your American competitors use daily. Without it, you're locked out. With it, you're in.

01

Open Your Client File

Fill out the quick form so we can open a request from the ITIN team to begin the process. We'll ask for info such as: Full Name, Address, Passport Scan, etc. Within 24–72 hours you'll get a call from an onboarding specialist ensuring accurate document submission.

02

Document Processing

The Certified Acceptance Agent notarizes your documents and sends back one last file to sign. Once complete we will directly mail the Texas IRS location with your details so we can get your ITIN.

03

Receive Your ITIN

Get emails & nuggets from the American Leverage team so you can establish a good banking score while you wait for your ITIN. Receive the ITIN within 6–12 weeks of the processing date. (IRS processing times vary)

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about American Leverage

Is it legal for non-U.S. citizens to access American credit?

Yes. It is completely legal for non-U.S. citizens to access U.S. credit as long as the proper process is followed. U.S. banks and credit issuers welcome international business owners.

Do I need to live in the U.S. to qualify?

No. You do not need to live in or relocate to the United States. The entire process is designed to work remotely for international business owners.

Do I need to pay U.S. taxes?

Having an ITIN does not automatically create a U.S. tax liability. Your obligations depend on your specific situation — we recommend consulting a qualified tax professional.

How long does it take to get results?

ITINs are typically issued within 6–12 weeks of processing. From there, many clients begin accessing U.S. credit within the following months.

Do I need to travel to the U.S.?

Travel is optional. The core process can be completed remotely, though some clients choose to travel to strengthen their banking relationships.

What is American Leverage Platinum?

Our premium done-for-you tier, where our specialists handle the heavy lifting end-to-end — from ITIN to premium card placement and funding strategy.

What are your offers?

We help you secure a U.S. ITIN, establish American credit, and unlock 0% interest funding and premium travel rewards — without relocating.

What do you help non-U.S. business owners achieve?

Access to 6-figure 0% credit lines, 4x points on business spend, and up to 95% savings on five-star travel — the same tools American competitors use daily.

What are the minimum requirements to qualify?

A valid passport, a business or strong intent to build one, and a willingness to follow the process. We guide you through every step.

Do I need an existing U.S. LLC or business entity before working with you?

Not necessarily. Many clients start without one — we'll advise on the right structure for your goals as part of the process.

How is my personal information protected?

Your documents are handled through secure, encrypted channels and shared only with the certified agents required to process your ITIN.

How do you support clients in the process?

You're assigned an onboarding specialist and receive ongoing guidance — from document submission to credit-building strategy — every step of the way.
